Since Day One

A kitchen built on trust

Fresh Food Junction opened in New Pawan Nagar, Amritsar, with a simple idea — fast food doesn't have to mean careless food. Every parantha is hand-rolled, every burger patty pressed to order, and every kitchen surface cleaned before the next ticket comes in.

Today we run a menu of over 140 dishes spanning North Indian breakfast classics, Indo-Chinese momos, stacked burgers, stone-baked pizza and hand-blended shakes — all cooked fresh, never pre-made, and available for takeaway or delivery through Zomato and Swiggy.
